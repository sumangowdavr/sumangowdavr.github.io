Project overview

The project addresses the following problem: Programmed a 6-DOF UFactory xArm 6 for vision-guided cube sorting: a camera detects and localizes cubes, and the arm plans and executes pick-and-place with grasp selection. Built and demonstrated in the DREAM Lab robotics workshops I led for undergraduates.
