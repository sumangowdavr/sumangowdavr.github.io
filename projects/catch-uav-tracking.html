The platform combines a Holybro X500, Cube Orange+ running PX4, a RealSense camera and a Raspberry Pi 5 companion computer. The research spans recorded-video replay, simulation and bounded outdoor tracking tests. The flight shown here demonstrates yaw tracking; it does not establish autonomous pursuit over arbitrary paths.

Ongoing research with ADAMS Lab, University at Buffalo. Manuscript in preparation.
