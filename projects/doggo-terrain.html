Project 03 / Robotics and autonomy

Doggo over uneven terrain

Footstep affordance and costmap work from a 3D occupancy octree. Slope features are extracted with PCA over height deviations, and spline-based end-effector trajectories select footholds that avoid obstacles. Built on the Stanford Doggo platform with a Jetson TX2 and a RealSense depth camera.
