02

Research Focus

Mechanical design, robot integration, and autonomous control.

I work on how robots are built, how they sense their surroundings, and how their control software turns that information into useful motion.

Decentralized multi-robot systems

Teams of robots moving a shared payload through geometry-based contact placement and local role decisions. Each robot computes its own command from shared pose and waypoint information. I study how those decisions change with payload shape, team size and inclined terrain.

Vision-based aerial autonomy

Camera-based tracking and image-based visual servoing, combining YOLO detections, correlation tracking and Kalman prediction. I built and flight-tested a PX4 drone that tracks another drone using only a camera and a Raspberry Pi 5 on board, and use Gazebo to study how detector timing and latency affect target visibility.

Simulation and hardware validation

Repeatable simulation scenarios, logged control behavior and physical robot demonstrations. I use each to examine where a controller works, where it fails, and what changes when an idealized model meets real contact and motion constraints.

Local trajectory optimization in the road-aligned Frenet frame using quintic/quartic polynomials for jerk-minimizing paths, with a risk-aware Gaussian cost and time-indexed collision checking for dynamic obstacles — about 16× safer time-to-collision than hard collision filtering while keeping the lowest jerk across six scenarios.
