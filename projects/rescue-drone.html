Project 04 / Robotics and autonomy

Modular rescue and surveillance drone

A quadcopter platform that can fly and drive using a servo mechanism that tilts the motor arms. LiDAR generates point clouds for 3D mapping in difficult spaces, and a modular attachment slot adapts the platform for different missions.
